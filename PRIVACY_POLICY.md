## 1. What ORBIT AI does
ORBIT AI is a personal assistant app. You can chat by text or voice, run phone commands, manage files you authorise, view device information, and use writing/study tools.

## 2. Data stored only on your device
- Chat history (can be switched off in Settings, and deleted at any time)
- Memories you approved
- Settings, custom commands, routines and automation history
- Offline AI model files you downloaded or imported
- The proxy access token you enter (stored encrypted using Android Keystore)
- The list of folders you authorised for file management

This data is not uploaded to the developer. We do not run analytics and do not include advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 3. Data sent off your device
When **online AI** answers, the app sends the following to the backend proxy URL **you configured**, which forwards it to the AI provider you selected (Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic Claude):
- The message you typed or the text recognised from your voice
- Recent messages of the current conversation
- Your approved memories, if "Use memory in AI prompts" is on
- Images or document text you attach
- Your custom instructions and chosen name

The developer of the app does not receive this data unless the developer also operates the proxy. If you run your own proxy, you control it. Each AI provider handles data under its own terms; free tiers of some providers may use prompts to improve their products, so avoid sending sensitive information. **Offline AI sends nothing off the device.**

Voice: speech recognition is performed by your phone's speech service (typically Google's). Depending on the language pack, audio may be processed on Google's servers. ORBIT AI does not record audio to storage.

Model downloads connect to the URL you enter.

## 4. Permissions and why
| Permission | Why | When asked |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone | Voice input and optional wake word | When you tap the mic or enable the wake word |
| Notifications | Reminders, timers, routines, wake-word status | When you first use them |
| Photos, videos, audio (media) | Show and organise your media | In Files |
| Usage access (special) | App usage statistics | Only if you open that setting |
| Foreground service (microphone) | Optional wake-word listening, always with a visible notification and Stop button | When you enable the wake word |
| Internet | Online AI and model download | Always |
Folders and documents are accessed only through the system file picker for the locations you choose.

## 5. Wake word
Optional and off by default. While on, a notification is shown and the microphone is used to listen for the word "ORBIT". Audio is not stored or sent by ORBIT AI itself.

## 6. File operations
ORBIT AI can move, copy and rename files in folders you authorised, after showing a preview and asking for confirmation. It does not delete files.

## 7. Your choices
Turn off history, memory and the wake word in Settings. Delete chats and memory in Settings. Revoke permissions in Android settings. Uninstalling removes all local data.
